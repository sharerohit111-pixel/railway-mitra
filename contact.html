Get in touch

Contact Railway Mitra

We welcome reports about calculation errors, broken links, accessibility issues, content corrections and general feedback.

Email support

For general questions, website feedback, correction requests or privacy-related enquiries, email us at railwaymitra.support111@gmail.com.

Public technical reports

For a public issue or technical report, use the Railway Mitra GitHub issue tracker. Please do not post employee numbers, personal documents, passwords, OTPs or other private information in a public issue.

For account-specific privacy or deletion requests, you may also use the Account & Data section available after signing in, as described in our Privacy Policy.

What to include in a correction request

  • The page or calculator name.
  • The relevant rule, circular or official source, if available.
  • The date and version of the source.
  • A short description of the issue, without personal data.
