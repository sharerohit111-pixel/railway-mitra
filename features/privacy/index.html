Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026 · Service: Railway Mitra

Railway Mitra is an independently developed digital companion offering railway-related information, employee tools, calculators and optional account-based features. This policy describes the information the app may process, why it is used, where it may be stored and the choices available to users.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Sign-in information: If you choose to sign in, Firebase Authentication processes the phone number used for OTP sign-in or information made available by your Google sign-in account.
  • Employee profile details: Details you choose to enter may include your name, post, pay level, basic pay, transport allowance, division, zone, employee number, department, official contact details and custom fields.
  • Duty and feature records: If you use account-based features, the app may process duty diary entries, attendance-related details, exam attempts, saved preferences and other records you submit or create.
  • Feedback: Feedback may include its category, rating, subject, message, replies and related timestamps.
  • Technical and security information: The app and its service providers may process information needed to authenticate requests, maintain service reliability, prevent misuse and diagnose technical problems.
  • Payment-related information: If a payment feature is enabled and you use it, the app may process payment request details and transaction evidence needed to verify or support that transaction. Do not submit card PINs, banking passwords or OTPs in free-text fields.

Some information is optional and is processed only if you use the related feature or choose to provide it. Avoid entering Aadhaar numbers, bank account credentials, passwords or other highly sensitive information in employee profile fields.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide sign-in, save and synchronize your records, calculate or display results you request, maintain your account, respond to feedback, verify transactions where applicable, protect the service and troubleshoot errors. Railway Mitra does not need your information for unrelated advertising profiles.

3. Local storage, cloud storage and service providers

Some calculator inputs, preferences, employee profile details or offline duty records may be stored in your browser or on your device using local browser storage. These local copies are not automatically erased merely because you sign out or delete an online account. You may need to clear app/browser data on each device where you used Railway Mitra.

For supported account features, Railway Mitra uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions. Information sent to these services is processed on Google's infrastructure under Google's applicable terms and privacy commitments. Offline records may be synchronized when connectivity returns and the relevant feature supports synchronization.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Railway Mitra does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by Google Firebase to operate supported app features. Feedback submissions and related conversations may be visible to authorized Railway Mitra administrators so they can respond. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the service or its security.

5. Retention and account deletion

You can review the Account & Data page. At present, the self-service account deletion option is not yet enabled. If you need to request account or data deletion before that option becomes available, contact the Railway Mitra team through the Feedback page and clearly state that it is a privacy/deletion request. The team may need to verify account ownership before acting.

When an account deletion service is available, certain transaction evidence may need to be retained in a minimized form for accounting, dispute resolution, fraud prevention or legal compliance. Information stored only on your device must be cleared separately on each device. Backups and technical logs may remain for a limited period according to operational and legal requirements.

6. Security

Supported cloud features use Firebase authentication and server-side access checks. Keep your device secure and never share OTPs or account credentials. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Railway Mitra is designed primarily for railway employees and users of employee-related services. It is not designed to target children.

8. Independent service disclaimer

Railway Mitra is independently developed. It is not an official Indian Railways, Ministry of Railways, CRIS or Government of India application and is not affiliated with, approved by or endorsed by those organizations. See the Independent App Disclaimer and Legal Information page.

9. Policy changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date will be revised when material changes are made. For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the Feedback page.

This policy is intended to explain the app's current and planned data practices in plain language. It should be checked against the live production configuration and applicable legal requirements before public release.
