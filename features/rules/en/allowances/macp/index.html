CATEGORY 09 · TOPIC 03

Modified Assured Career Progression (MACP)

Financial up-gradation intervals, nature, APAR benchmark and deferment.

MACP Scheme Intervals

Financial up-gradation is described at 10, 20 and 30 years of continuous regular service, provided no regular promotion has been achieved within the respective decades.

Nature of Up-gradation

MACP provides a purely financial up-gradation to the immediate next higher Level in the 7th CPC Pay Matrix. It does not change functional designation, responsibilities or seniority status.

Performance Benchmarking · APAR

Up-gradation is subject to fitness evaluation based on Annual Performance Appraisal Reports (APAR). The stated benchmark is at least “Very Good” continuously for all the preceding five years.

Effect of Penalties & Deferment

If an employee has an active major/minor penalty under D&AR rules or is rated below the benchmark, the up-gradation date is described as deferred for the currency period of the penalty or until fitness is achieved.
