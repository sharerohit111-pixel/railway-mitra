Running Allowance Rules

Rules and allowance types described for Loco Pilots and Train Guards.

Kilometrage Allowance (KMA)

Paid to running staff based on the actual physical kilometres performed during a trip. The rate is calculated per 100 km under the 7th CPC framework and varies by designation, such as Mail/Express Loco Pilot, Goods Guard and Shunting Pilot.

Allowance in Lieu of Kilometrage (ALK)

Paid when running staff are deployed on stationary duties, such as attending training, waiting for duty, medical examinations or suspended execution of regular trips. It is generally calculated at a fixed standard rate of 160 km per day.

Breach of Rest Allowance

When a running staff member is recalled to duty before completing the mandatory standard rest period at the home station, additional compensation is calculated on an hourly basis.

Outstation Detention Allowance

Applicable when running staff are detained at an outstation lobby or running room beyond a continuous block of 16 hours from the time they signed off. Compensation increases progressively for subsequent hours.
