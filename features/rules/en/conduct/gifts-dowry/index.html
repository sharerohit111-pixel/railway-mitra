CATEGORY 08 · CONDUCT RULES

Gifts, Dowry & Hospitality

Gift limits, reporting requirements, commercial hospitality and dowry prohibition

  1. Acceptance of gifts (Rule 11): Gifts from near relatives or personal friends on solemn occasions such as weddings, anniversaries or funerals are subject to monetary ceilings.
  2. Near relatives — no report required below the stated limits: Group A officers ₹25,000; Group B officers ₹15,000; Group C staff ₹7,500.
  3. Personal friends with no official dealings — no report required below the stated limits: Group A and B officers ₹5,000; Group C staff ₹2,000.
  4. Reporting: Any gift exceeding the applicable amount must be reported to the Government within 30 days of receipt.
  5. Commercial hospitality and official gifts: Lavish, frequent or expensive hospitality or gifts from commercial firms, contractors, suppliers or persons having active official business dealings with the Railways are barred.
  6. Dowry (Rule 13-A): A railway servant must not give, take or abet the giving or taking of dowry, and must not directly or indirectly demand dowry from the parents or guardians of a bride or bridegroom.
  7. The guide states that a dowry-rule violation triggers major penalty disciplinary proceedings.
