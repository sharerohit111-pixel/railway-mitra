CATEGORY 08 · CONDUCT RULES

Private Trade, Political Activities & Strikes

Outside employment, honorary work, political association, elections and demonstrations

  1. Private trade and employment (Rule 15): A railway servant must not directly or indirectly engage in trade or business, or undertake other employment or profession outside official duties.
  2. Honorary work: Social or charitable work or holding an honorary office requires prior written intimation to the administration, provided it does not interfere with official duties.
  3. Political association and activities (Rule 5): A railway servant must not be a member of, or otherwise associated with, a political party or an organisation that takes part in politics.
  4. Election restrictions: A railway servant must not canvass, influence, organise vote-pooling, interfere with an election to a legislature or local authority, or use official authority in an election.
  5. Demonstrations and strikes (Rule 7): A railway servant must not engage in or incite a strike, demonstration or mass casual leave that disrupts public safety or the normal operation of railway services.
