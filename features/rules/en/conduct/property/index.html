CATEGORY 08 · CONDUCT RULES

Property Declaration & Transactions

Annual property return, immovable and movable transactions, disclosure and speculation

  1. Annual Property Return (APR): Every railway servant holding a Group A, Group B or Group C post must submit an annual return of immovable property inherited, owned or acquired.
  2. Submission deadline: The guide states that the return is to be filed electronically through HRMS by 31 January each year for the preceding calendar year.
  3. Immovable property transactions (Rule 18(2)): Prior written sanction of the competent authority (GM/DRM) is required where the transaction is with a person or party having official dealings with the railway servant.
  4. Where an immovable property transaction is through a regular authorised dealer or reputed developer and there are no official dealings, prior intimation must be submitted at least 30 days before executing the deed.
  5. Movable property transactions (Rule 18(3)): Report to the administration within 30 days if the total transaction value exceeds ₹20,000 for Group A and B officers, or ₹15,000 for Group C staff.
  6. Benami and joint ownership: Property purchased in the name of a family member or dependent using the employee's own funds must be explicitly disclosed.
  7. Speculation: High-frequency speculative stock-market trading or speculative real-estate flipping is prohibited.
