Punishment Hierarchy & Penalty Matrix

Minor and major penalties described under Rule 6

  1. The guide separates penalties into minor and major categories and says the chargesheet track limits the punishment that may be imposed.
  2. Minor penalties listed: Censure; withholding of promotion; recovery of pecuniary loss; withholding of Privilege Passes/PTOs; reduction by one stage in the time-scale for up to 3 years without cumulative effect; withholding increments without cumulative effect.
  3. Major penalties listed: withholding increments with cumulative effect; reduction to a lower stage in time-scale; reduction to a lower post, grade, service or level; compulsory retirement; removal from service; dismissal from service.
  4. The guide describes removal as not automatically disqualifying a person from applying for other future government or public-sector employment, while dismissal is described as carrying permanent disqualification. Verify the actual legal effect from the applicable rules and order.
