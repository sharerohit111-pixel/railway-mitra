CATEGORY 10 · TOPIC 04

Extraordinary Leave & Leave Encashment

Extraordinary Leave, encashment during active service and at retirement.

Extraordinary Leave (EOL)

Granted when no other regular leave is admissible or when the employee requests EOL in writing. No salary or allowances are paid during EOL. The supplied text states that the period does not count for increment or pension calculations except when taken on certified medical grounds.

Encashment During Active Service

Up to 10 days of LAP may be encashed at a time, once in a block of two years. The stated lifetime ceiling during active service is 60 days. At least 30 days of LAP must remain after deduction, and encashment must be availed concurrently with a valid Privilege Pass or PTO.

Encashment at Retirement

At retirement, the stated lump-sum cash equivalent is for accumulated LAP up to 300 days. If LAP is less than 300 days, accumulated LHAP may be used to make up the shortfall to the stated maximum.
