CATEGORY 06 · MEDICAL

Medical Decategorization Rules

Alternative safe duties, service protection and pay protection

  1. Medical decategorization occurs when an employee in a safety or running category (such as Loco Pilot, Assistant Loco Pilot, Guard or Trackman) is declared medically unfit for the original medical classification (for example, A-1 or A-2) during a Periodical Medical Examination (PME) or due to illness or an accident, and is withdrawn from that duty.
  2. Job protection: Under the RPWD Act and Railway Board guidelines, an employee cannot be terminated or dismissed solely because they have been declared medically unfit for their original post.
  3. Pay protection: The employee is to be absorbed in an alternative safe post or desk job, such as a clerical or administrative post, matching the employee's new medical fitness level. The original basic pay, grade pay and scale remain protected.
  4. Supernumerary post: If a suitable alternative post is not immediately vacant, a temporary supernumerary post is created so the employee can continue to draw full salary until a permanent alternative vacancy becomes available.
  5. Running allowance adjustment: For decategorized running staff (Loco Pilots and Guards), a component representing running allowance (for example, 30% added to basic pay) is factored into pay fixation on the alternative post.

Confirm current applicability with the competent Railway Medical Authority before acting on these provisions.
