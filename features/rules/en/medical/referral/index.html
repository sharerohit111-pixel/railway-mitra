CATEGORY 06 · MEDICAL

Referral Hospital Policy

Specialist treatment, emergency admission and reimbursement process

  1. When specialised treatment, advanced procedures or diagnostic facilities are not available at the local Railway Hospital, the authorised Medical Officer refers the patient to a higher medical centre or an empanelled private hospital.
  2. In an acute life-threatening emergency, such as sudden cardiac arrest, a severe road accident, coma or acute respiratory failure, the patient may be admitted directly to the nearest recognised private or empanelled hospital without prior referral.
  3. In an emergency admission, written or digital intimation must be submitted to the concerned Railway Medical Officer (CMS/MD) within 48 hours of admission.
  4. For reimbursement, the claimant must submit an application along with original bills, discharge summary, emergency certificate and detailed case reports.
  5. The Railway Medical Committee evaluates the medical urgency and sanctions reimbursement based on approved CGHS/Railway rates.

Confirm current applicability with the competent Railway Medical Authority before acting on these provisions.
