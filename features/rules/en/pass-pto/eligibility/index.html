CATEGORY 03 · PASS & PTO

Basic Definitions & General Eligibility

Family, dependent relatives, validity and attendant rules

  1. For Pass and PTO benefits, the PDF defines family to include the spouse, dependent sons under 21, and unmarried, widowed or legally divorced daughters.
  2. Widowed mothers, dependent unmarried or divorced sisters, and dependent minor brothers under 21 may be included if wholly dependent and the father is not alive.
  3. A dependent relative's monthly income from all sources must not exceed 15% of the railway servant's Basic Pay or ₹9,000 + DA, whichever is higher, as stated in the PDF.
  4. If only immediate family is included, the PDF states there is no upper member-count limit. If dependent relatives are included, their number is capped at 2 and total pass capacity at 5, excluding an authorised attendant.
  5. The PDF states that HRMS e-Passes and PTOs are valid for 5 months from issue.
  6. First Class 'A' pass holders may take one personal salaried domestic servant/attendant in Second Class/Sleeper at no additional cost, as stated in the PDF.

Source: Railway_Pass_and_PTO_Rules_Final.pdf, pages 1–2. This reflects the uploaded PDF and is not an independently verified statement of current rules.
