HRMS Process & Misuse Penalties

Digital pass process, prohibited misuse and penalties

  1. The PDF states that physical pass ledgers are obsolete and that applications, tracking, routing modifications and booking links are handled through the HRMS e-Pass module.
  2. The PDF describes selling or transferring passes, or declaring false dependency details to obtain passes for unauthorised persons, as a major offence that may attract major penalties under DAR Rules, including removal or dismissal.
  3. The PDF mentions administrative fines of ₹5 to ₹35 per validation lapse for misplacing or failing to log active journey parameters on manual passes where emergency configurations still apply.

Source: Railway_Pass_and_PTO_Rules_Final.pdf, pages 1–2. This reflects the uploaded PDF and is not an independently verified statement of current rules.
