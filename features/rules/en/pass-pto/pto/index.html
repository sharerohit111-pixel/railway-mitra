Privilege Ticket Order (PTO) Rules

Annual PTO entitlement, fare concession and attendant restriction

  1. The PDF states that every serving employee, irrespective of service length or Group A/B/C/D classification, receives 4 Full Sets of PTO per calendar year.
  2. Under a PTO, the employee or family member pays one-third (33.3%) of the base fare; the remaining two-thirds (66.6%) is subsidised by the Railways, as stated in the PDF.
  3. A PTO does not authorise a complimentary or subsidised personal attendant in any travel class.

Source: Railway_Pass_and_PTO_Rules_Final.pdf, pages 1–2. This reflects the uploaded PDF and is not an independently verified statement of current rules.
