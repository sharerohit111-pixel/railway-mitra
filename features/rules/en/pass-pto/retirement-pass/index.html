Post-Retirement Complimentary Pass (PRCP)

Qualifying service, annual sets and senior companion

  1. The PDF states a minimum of 20 years of regular, continuous Railway service to qualify for lifetime complimentary post-retirement pass benefits.
  2. Non-Gazetted Group C & D: 20–25 years of service = 1 Set per year; more than 25 years = 2 Sets per year, as stated in the PDF.
  3. Gazetted Group A & B: 20–25 years of service = 2 Sets per year; more than 25 years = 3 Sets per year, as stated in the PDF.
  4. The PDF says retired employees aged 65 or above holding First Class post-retirement passes may take a companion in Sleeper/Second Class if travelling without other family members.

Source: Railway_Pass_and_PTO_Rules_Final.pdf, pages 1–2. This reflects the uploaded PDF and is not an independently verified statement of current rules.
