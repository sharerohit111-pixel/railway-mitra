School Pass Entitlement

Eligibility, annual limit and guardian certificate

  1. The PDF states that a School Pass is granted when a dependent child studies regularly at a recognised boarding school or university away from the employee's official station.
  2. The PDF lists a maximum of 3 Full Sets or 6 Half Sets per academic year for vacation travel between the educational institution and the parent's station.
  3. For a minor student or a female child, the PDF says a Guardian/Parent Certificate may be added to authorise a parent or escort to travel free alongside the student.

Source: Railway_Pass_and_PTO_Rules_Final.pdf, pages 1–2. This reflects the uploaded PDF and is not an independently verified statement of current rules.
