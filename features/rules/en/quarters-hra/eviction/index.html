CATEGORY 07 · QUARTERS & HRA

Eviction, Penal Rent & Unauthorized Occupation

  1. Subletting: The document states that fully or partially subletting a railway quarter to outsiders is prohibited.
  2. If subletting is established, the document describes cancellation of allotment, summary eviction under the Public Premises (Eviction of Unauthorized Occupants) Act, 1971, and major disciplinary proceedings.
  3. Unauthorized occupation: Staying beyond the authorized retention period is treated as unauthorized occupation.
  4. Damage rent: The document describes damage rent calculated per square metre of plinth area, with an example of up to ₹5,860 per month for large Type-V properties, subject to periodic revision.
  5. Gratuity: The document states that the administration may withhold retirement gratuity (DCRG) until the quarter is handed over if it is not vacated after retirement or transfer.
  6. Privilege Passes and PTOs: The document states that post-retirement Privilege Passes and PTOs are forfeited during the unauthorized occupation period.
  7. Eviction notice: A formal notice under the Public Premises Act, 1971 may be issued and estate officers may clear the premises.
