How to Organise a DA Arrear Check

A dearness allowance (DA) arrear estimate usually covers a period during which the applicable DA rate changed or an earlier payment needs to be reconciled. The difficult part is often not arithmetic: it is identifying the correct rate, effective date, pay basis and months already paid.

Collect the source records first

Keep the relevant pay slips, basic-pay history, promotion or increment orders, and the official DA rate order for each period. If the estimate relates to a correction in payroll, also keep the office communication or representation that explains the issue.

Split the timeline into rate periods

Prepare a month-by-month timeline. Mark the effective date of each revised rate and split the claim whenever the rate or the employee's basic pay changes. Do not apply one rate to the whole period merely because the arrear is being calculated on one date.

Build a reconciliation sheet

  1. Write each month or part-period in a separate row.
  2. Record the basic pay applicable for that period.
  3. Record the DA rate and the official order supporting it.
  4. Compare the amount due with the amount actually credited.
  5. Show the difference separately and total only after checking every row.

Common causes of differences

Differences can arise from a wrong effective month, a pay change within the period, rounding, leave or other service events, payroll adjustments, or an amount already paid under a separate entry. A calculator cannot determine which payroll treatment applies without the underlying records.

Before submitting a query

Attach a concise month-wise statement and copies of relevant orders or pay slips. State the exact period and explain the assumption that needs checking. Ask the dealing office to confirm the applicable rate, pay basis and any adjustment already made.
