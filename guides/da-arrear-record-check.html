How to Organise a DA Arrear Check

A DA arrear check compares the DA that should apply to a historical pay period with the DA already credited for that same period. Accuracy depends on the Basic Pay, applicable rate, effective date, payroll treatment and any amount already paid. Do not calculate the entire claim using today's pay or rate.

1. Collect the records

  • Pay slips for every month in the period being checked.
  • Basic Pay history and relevant promotion, increment or pay-fixation orders.
  • Railway Board DA orders and any separate arrears instructions.
  • Payroll statements or office communications showing amounts already credited.
  • Records of leave, service changes or other events that may affect the pay period.

2. Make a rate timeline

List every DA rate and its effective date from the official orders. The Railway Board's 2026 orders listing records the DA revision effective from 01.07.2026, and the RBE No. 91/2026 order specifies 63% of Basic Pay from that date. Use the order for each earlier or later period rather than assuming one rate applies throughout.

3. Prepare a month-wise worksheet

  1. Enter the pay month and the Basic Pay applicable in that month.
  2. Record the DA percentage and the order/effective date supporting it.
  3. Calculate the expected DA using the applicable payroll method.
  4. Record the DA amount actually credited in the pay slip.
  5. Show the difference as a separate figure.
  6. Record any arrear payment, adjustment or recovery already made.

4. Handle changes within the period carefully

If Basic Pay changes because of an increment, promotion or pay fixation, use the correct pay basis for the affected month or part-period according to the authorised payroll treatment. If an order becomes effective part-way through a month, do not assume the whole month is treated identically; check the wording of the order and the accounts-office method.

5. Check rounding and previous payments

Read the rounding provision in each applicable order. For example, RBE No. 91/2026 states that DA fractions of 50 paise and above may be rounded to the next higher rupee and fractions below 50 paise may be ignored. Also check whether arrears have already been credited separately so that the same amount is not claimed twice.

6. Explain a discrepancy clearly

Prepare a short statement identifying the month, Basic Pay, rate, expected amount, amount credited and difference. Attach only relevant supporting records through the authorised channel. Ask the dealing office to confirm the applicable rate, pay basis and any prior adjustment.

Official references

  • Railway Board – DA and Establishment orders listing
  • RBE No. 91/2026 – DA revised to 63% from 01.07.2026 (PDF)
  • Indian Railway Establishment Code – Chapter 14

Educational information only. This guide does not determine arrear entitlement or replace payroll verification. The applicable order, employee's service record and authorised accounts-office calculation prevail.
