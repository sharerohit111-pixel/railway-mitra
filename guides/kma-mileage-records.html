Mileage and duty records

Kilometre Allowance: Keeping a Reliable Mileage Record

A kilometre-based allowance estimate is only meaningful when the underlying distance and duty records are correct. The applicable entitlement and rate depend on current official instructions and the employee's circumstances; a mileage figure alone is not a payment approval.

Record each journey or duty segment

Use the prescribed register or approved record for the relevant work. Capture the date, duty purpose, start and end points, kilometre reading or authorised distance, and the supporting order or entry reference. Keep separate entries for separate trips or segments where the record system requires it.

Verify the distance source

Use the official distance reference or measurement method accepted by the administration. Do not substitute a consumer map route, straight-line distance or personal odometer reading unless the applicable process explicitly accepts it. Explain any correction to an earlier entry.

Check eligibility and rate period

Confirm that the duty and employee category are covered by the current rule. Record the effective date of the applicable rate and check whether the period crosses a rate change. Also verify any prescribed minimum, maximum, rounding or other condition directly from the official order.

Reconcile month-wise

  1. Total the accepted kilometres by date or duty category.
  2. Compare the total with the authorised register.
  3. Apply only the rate and method valid for that period.
  4. Compare the estimate with the amount credited.
  5. Keep disputed or unsupported entries separate from verified entries.

Make the record easy to audit

Use consistent units, avoid overwriting original entries and retain correction references. A clear date-wise record helps the supervisor or accounts office trace the source of a difference quickly.

Educational information only. This article is not an official railway order, sanction, safety instruction or eligibility decision. Always verify the current applicable Railway Board, zonal and divisional instructions and consult the authorised office.
