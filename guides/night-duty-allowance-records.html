Duty allowance guide

Night Duty Allowance: Records to Check

Night duty allowance (NDA) is a duty-related payment governed by applicable rules and instructions. An employee should not estimate it from a simple count of nights alone. The roster, actual duty record, eligible hours, pay details and current instructions may all matter.

Start with the duty evidence

Collect the approved roster, attendance or muster record, duty allocation and any correction to the original entry. Check that the dates and shift timings match the period being reviewed. If a shift crosses midnight, preserve the actual start and end times rather than recording only one calendar date.

Confirm the applicable rule

Find the current official instructions that apply to the employee's category and period. Check the definition of night hours, any qualifying conditions, exclusions, ceilings and the method used by the payroll office. Confirm whether later circulars changed the treatment.

Check pay and service details

Use the pay information required by the applicable order and verify that it belongs to the same period as the duty records. A later promotion, increment or pay revision should not automatically be applied retrospectively to every earlier month.

Reconcile the estimate

  1. List each relevant duty date and shift timing.
  2. Identify the qualifying portion under the applicable rule.
  3. Record the official rate or calculation method and its effective date.
  4. Compare the estimated amount with the pay slip entry.
  5. Flag missing duty entries or unexplained differences for office verification.

Keep the calculation transparent

Save the source order, the duty period and the assumptions used. If the roster and attendance record disagree, resolve the record discrepancy before relying on the total. Do not treat an online estimate as proof that a payment is admissible.

Educational information only. This article is not an official railway order, sanction, safety instruction or eligibility decision. Always verify the current applicable Railway Board, zonal and divisional instructions and consult the authorised office.
