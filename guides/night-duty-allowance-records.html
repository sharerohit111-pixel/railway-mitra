Duty allowance guide

Night Duty Allowance: Records to Check

Night Duty Allowance (NDA) is governed by Railway Board instructions and eligibility conditions. A count of night shifts by itself does not establish entitlement or the amount payable. Check the employee category, qualifying duty, period, pay details and applicable orders together.

1. Understand the published rule framework

Railway Board's RBE No. 85/2022 (PDF) clarifies that NDA may be granted to eligible non-gazetted Railway employees up to Pay Level 7, including employees granted the benefit of MACP in Pay Level 8. It also states that the Basic Pay ceiling used for working out the hourly rate remains ₹43,600. These are not the only conditions: the employee's category and other applicable instructions must also be checked.

2. Check the night-duty period and certification

The Railway Board instruction refers to work performed between 22:00 and 06:00 and continues the weightage of 10 minutes for every hour of duty performed in that period. The earlier RBE No. 83/2020 text also refers to certification by the supervisor that night duty is essential. Verify the current applicable order and local process before using these details in a claim or estimate.

3. Assemble the supporting records

  • Approved duty roster and any revised roster.
  • Attendance, muster or other authorised record of actual duty.
  • Shift start and end times, especially where duty crosses midnight.
  • Supervisor's required certification or approval.
  • Basic Pay and DA applicable to the relevant month, supported by the pay slip or authorised pay record.
  • Any order affecting eligibility, pay ceiling or calculation for the period.

4. Reconcile one month at a time

  1. Separate each pay month and list the actual duty dates.
  2. Identify only the hours falling within the rule-defined night period.
  3. Check the employee's eligibility and the Basic Pay ceiling for that period.
  4. Apply the calculation method and weightage stated in the applicable order; do not substitute an unofficial formula.
  5. Compare the estimate with the NDA entry in the pay slip and note differences.
  6. Ask the authorised office to resolve missing or conflicting roster and attendance records.

5. Avoid common errors

Do not count an entire shift as night duty merely because part of it overlaps the night period. Do not use today's Basic Pay or DA rate for an earlier month without checking the historical record. Do not assume all staff categories qualify, and do not treat a calculator result as proof of sanction.

Official references

  • Railway Board – RBE No. 85/2022, NDA clarification (PDF)
  • RBE No. 83/2020 – earlier NDA instruction (secondary hosted copy)

This guide is for education and record organisation only, not an eligibility decision or payment sanction. Check the latest Railway Board, zonal and divisional instructions with the authorised personnel/accounts office before relying on an estimate.
