Salary record guide

How to Review a Railway Pay Slip

A pay slip is a monthly record of earnings and deductions. A structured review can help you spot a missing entry, an unexpected recovery or a change that needs an explanation. The exact entries depend on your post, service record, duty and applicable orders.

1. Read earnings and deductions separately

Basic Pay is the pay drawn in the applicable Pay Matrix level and cell. Gross earnings combine Basic Pay with admissible allowances and other earnings. Net Pay is what remains after deductions. Do not compare net pay alone: a change in deductions can alter take-home pay even when earnings have not changed.

2. Verify Basic Pay against service events

Keep the relevant pay-fixation statement, increment order, promotion order or other authorised service record. Check the effective date and whether the pay slip reflects the correct level and cell in the correct month. If an event is effective part-way through a period, ask the authorised office how the payroll period and any arrears have been treated.

3. Check DA and other variable allowances

Dearness Allowance (DA) is notified through government and Railway Board instructions and changes by effective date. Use the order applicable to the pay month, not a rate remembered from a later month. The Railway Board's 2026 orders listing includes the DA revision effective from 01.07.2026 (RBE No. 91/2026, dated 30.10.2026) and a subsequent arrears order dated 05.11.2026. Open the actual order and verify the rate, effective date and applicability before calculating.

Duty-linked or location-linked entries should be checked against the roster, attendance, kilometre or other authorised record and the relevant rule. Do not assume an allowance is payable simply because it appeared on a previous month's slip.

4. Review deductions and recoveries

Compare each deduction with the relevant loan, subscription, contribution, tax or recovery record available to you. Check whether a deduction is recurring or one-time, whether its amount changed, and whether an adjustment or arrear recovery is separately listed. If the description is unclear, request the deduction details from the authorised payroll or accounts office.

5. Use a month-by-month comparison

  1. Confirm employee details and the pay period.
  2. Compare Basic Pay with the latest authorised pay record.
  3. Verify DA and other rate-based items against orders effective in that month.
  4. Match duty-linked earnings with approved duty or mileage records.
  5. Check deductions and recoveries against their source documents.
  6. Reconcile total earnings, total deductions and net pay.
  7. Keep a note of any unexplained difference and the office response.

6. Raise a precise query safely

State the pay month, exact pay-slip line, amount shown, what you expected and the supporting order or record. Use the authorised departmental channel. When asking for general help online, hide your employee number, bank details, address, QR codes and other personal identifiers.

Official starting points

  • Railway Board – 2026 Establishment/DA orders listing
  • Indian Railway Establishment Code – Chapter 14: Dearness and other compensatory allowances

Educational information only. A pay slip review does not replace an official pay-fixation statement, sanction or accounts-office decision. Always use the order and service record applicable to the specific month.
