Permanent way learning guide

Track Maintenance: Using Official References

Permanent Way work must be planned and carried out under the current Indian Railways manuals, approved procedures and instructions issued by the competent authority. This guide explains how to locate and verify a reference; it is not a method statement or permission to carry out work.

1. Identify the exact subject

Before searching a manual, describe the issue precisely: rails and welds, sleepers and fastenings, ballast, track geometry, points and crossings, patrolling, protection of work, inspection or emergency response. The subject helps you locate the relevant chapter, paragraph, table or drawing instead of relying on a broad search result.

2. Start with the current IRPWM

Use the Railway Board Codes and Manuals page to locate the Indian Railways Permanent Way Manual (IRPWM)-2024. Check the manual's edition and the correction-slip list shown by the official source. A saved PDF or printed copy may not include later amendments.

3. Verify correction slips and instructions

For the paragraph you intend to consult, record its number and check subsequent Advance Correction Slips (ACS), Railway Board letters, zonal circulars and divisional instructions. For example, Railway Board Correction Slip No. 3 to IRPWM-2024 demonstrates why the original text alone may not be the complete current reference. Confirm whether later slips supersede or further amend it.

4. Check the local application

Track category, line and speed conditions, traffic, block or traffic-protection arrangements, weather, site constraints and local instructions can affect what is authorised. Do not infer a safe work procedure from a general article. Obtain the current approved instruction and the required protection and supervision for the actual site.

5. Keep a reference record

  1. Write down the manual title, edition and relevant paragraph, table or drawing.
  2. Record the date on which you checked the official correction-slip list.
  3. Note any Railway Board, zonal or divisional instruction that applies.
  4. Confirm the authorised protection and work arrangements with the responsible supervisor.
  5. If documents conflict, appear incomplete or do not match site conditions, stop and seek clarification through the authorised chain.

Useful official starting points

  • Railway Board – Codes and Manuals / IRPWM-2024
  • Railway Board – Correction Slip No. 3 (PDF)
